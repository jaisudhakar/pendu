Made to feel real

Physics first. Pixels second.

Nine ropes, nine feels

Each rope is its own set of solver values, not just a different picture. A thread floats and flutters, leather keeps its curve, and a gold chain hangs heavy and settles slowly.

A real rope, simulated

A Verlet solver runs at a fixed 240 Hz with twenty segments. It's independent of your screen's refresh rate, and the rope never stretches past 1.02× its length.

Up to three charms

Stack up to three charms on one cord in any order. Each has its own size and weight, so the stack swings like one.

Make your own

Drop in a PNG, JPG or SVG and it becomes a charm with the same hanging physics as the built-in ones.

Out of your way

The overlay is click-through everywhere except the charm itself, so you can keep working right under it.

Featherweight

When the charm settles, the solver goes to sleep and stops using the CPU until you touch it again.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no network calls. Your charms and settings stay on your machine.

Questions

Good to know

Is Pendu really free?

Yes. It's free on Ubuntu, Windows and macOS, and the source code is open. There's no paid tier and no ads.

Will it slow my computer down?

No. The physics only runs while the charm moves. Once it settles, the solver sleeps and the app does almost nothing until you touch it again.

Where does the charm hang?

From the top edge of your screen, just under the menu bar or top panel. You can move the anchor left or right, change the rope length, and pick the charm size in the app.

Can I click things under the charm?

Yes. The overlay lets clicks pass through everywhere except the charm itself.

Can I use my own images?

Yes. Open Create and drop a PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP. It becomes a charm with a jump ring, and you can hang it like any other.

Does it collect any data?

No. Pendu makes no network requests. Settings are saved in a small file in your user folder.

How do I quit or open settings?

Use the Pendu icon in the system tray (Windows and Ubuntu) or the menu bar (macOS). It has Library, Nudge, Pause, Start at login and Quit.

Why "Pendu"?

It's French for "hanging". That's all it does, and it tries to do it beautifully.
